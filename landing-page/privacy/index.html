Privacy Policy

AgentDoctor is an open-source developer tool. The project is designed to keep repository scans local, deterministic, and free of telemetry.

Scope

This policy applies to the AgentDoctor website and the AgentDoctor CLI project maintained at github.com/youssefsz/agentdoctor .

Website data

The website is a static site. It does not include account creation, first-party analytics, advertising pixels, tracking cookies, or forms. Your browser may still send standard request information, such as IP address, user agent, referring page, and request time, to the hosting provider that serves the site.

Support email

If you email support, the project receives your email address and the content you choose to send. That information is used to respond to the request and maintain project support history.

CLI scans

AgentDoctor scans local repository files and prints reports on your machine. During scans, AgentDoctor does not call AI APIs, send telemetry, or access the network. It is built to avoid printing secret values from environment files or MCP configuration.

Third-party links

The website links to GitHub and related project resources. Those sites are governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes

This policy may be updated when website hosting, support practices, or AgentDoctor behavior changes materially. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

Privacy and support questions can be sent to dhibi.ywsf@gmail.com.
